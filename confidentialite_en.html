Privacy policy — Crystal Ball: Fortune Telling

Effective date: November 3, 2026 (version 1.0.12)

This privacy policy describes how the Android application published under the following names — « Boule de cristal : Voyance » (fr), « Crystal Ball: Fortune Telling » (en), « Bola de cristal: Videncia » (es-ES and es-419), « Bola de cristal: Vidência » (pt-BR), « Kristallkugel: Wahrsagen » (de), « Sfera di cristallo: Veggenza » (it) — package com.kuriocie.boule_voyance, published by Kuriocie, handles user data.

Note: the name shown under the app icon on the device (Android android:label / application label in version 1.0.12) is « Boule de cristal : Voyance » in all languages. The names listed above are those used on Google Play for each language.

The application is offered for entertainment purposes only. The visions shown are not professional, medical, legal, financial or psychological advice.

1. Data collected and advertising

The application does not require an account and does not offer in-app purchases. The visions are included in the application; an Internet connection is used only for advertisements and for the consent message.

The application integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), version 25.4.0, and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK, version 4.0.0, both provided by Google. It offers a rewarded video ad, shown only if the user chooses it, to unlock the 3rd vision of the day. The application does not display any banner and does not impose any full-screen interstitial ad. If no ad is available, the application may present another of our applications, « Lampe magique : Génie Voyance » (Magic Lamp: Fortune Genie).

In this context, Google (AdMob / UMP) may collect and share:

  • the advertising ID and the App Set ID;
  • the IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • interactions with the app (for example ad views and clicks);
  • diagnostics and crash logs;
  • device and app information;
  • cookies or similar identifiers.

These data are used for advertising (including personalized advertising where consent allows), analytics and measurement, and fraud prevention and security. They are shared with Google as a third party. Data in transit are encrypted. For more information:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure for Google Play: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a Google consent message (UMP) is shown on first launch. This choice can be changed at any time from the in-app privacy menu (icon with tooltip « Privacy » in English; the entry to reopen the consent form is labelled « Privacy choices »). Labels in the other app languages: fr « Confidentialité » / « Choix de confidentialité »; es-ES « Privacidad » / « Ajustes de privacidad »; es-419 « Privacidad » / « Opciones de privacidad »; pt-BR « Privacidade » / « Opções de privacidade »; de « Datenschutz » / « Datenschutzeinstellungen »; it « Privacy » / « Scelte sulla privacy ». The user can also reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

Apart from the data processed by Google for advertising, Kuriocie itself does not collect any personal data. Kuriocie does not ask for and does not collect any:

  • name;
  • email address;
  • phone number;
  • postal address;
  • location data (other than the approximate location Google may derive from the IP address for ads);
  • contacts;
  • user account identifier.

The application does not provide a free-text input field: no message or content written by the user is collected or transmitted by Kuriocie.

2. Preferences stored on the device

A few preferences (sound on or off, feminine or masculine wording, relationship status used to adapt some visions, visions already revealed for the day and the current date, disclaimer already seen, language chosen in the menu) are stored only on the device (via Android SharedPreferences). They are never sent to Kuriocie or to a third party by the application itself and are deleted when the application is uninstalled or when its data are cleared in Android settings.

3. Permissions

The application does not request sensitive permissions such as precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or SMS. Version 1.0.12 declares the following permissions in the merged Android manifest:

  • INTERNET — to load ads and the Google consent message;
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to check whether a network is available before requesting an ad;
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — to access the advertising ID used by Google Mobile Ads;
  • ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS — Android Ad Services / Privacy Sandbox features used by the ads SDK for advertising identifiers, attribution and topics;
  • WAKE_LOCK — to keep the device awake briefly while ads or related background work run (brought by Google / AndroidX components);
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE — used by AndroidX WorkManager components shipped with the ads stack for short background tasks;
  • DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION (app-specific) — an internal AndroidX safeguard for broadcast receivers; it is not used to collect user data.

The « Privacy policy » menu item opens this page in the phone’s browser; that visit is then handled by the browser and by the page host (GitHub Pages), not by the application.

4. Sharing and sale of data

Kuriocie does not sell any data. The only data shared are those collected by Google (AdMob / UMP) for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, as described in section 1; Google may process them outside the European Union under its own privacy rules.

5. User rights

Where the GDPR or an equivalent law applies, the user has rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country (for example the CNIL in France). For EU / EEA authorities, see the official list of the European Data Protection Board: https://www.edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en.

Because the application does not transmit personal data to Kuriocie, Kuriocie holds no personal data about its users. For data processed by Google, the user can change the consent choice in the in-app privacy menu and consult https://policies.google.com/privacy. For any question, write to: kuriocie23@gmail.com.

6. Minors

The application is intended for adults (18 years and older) and is not designed for minors. Kuriocie itself does not collect any personal data, including about minors.

7. Security

Preferences stay in the application’s private storage on the device, protected by the Android system. Exchanges with Google’s advertising services are encrypted in transit.

8. No important decisions

The predictions shown by « Crystal Ball: Fortune Telling » are offered for entertainment purposes only. They must not be used as a basis for important decisions about health, safety, finances, law, employment or personal relationships.

9. Changes to this privacy policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the application, features added or removed, or regulatory developments. In case of a material change, the new version will be published at the same address; the effective date at the top identifies the version in force.
