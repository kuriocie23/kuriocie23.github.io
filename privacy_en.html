Privacy Policy — Magic Lamp: Genie Answers

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy applies to the Android app “Magic Lamp: Genie Answers” (package com.kuriocie.lampe_genie), published by Kuriocie. The app is an entertainment game intended only for people aged 18 and over. The genie's answers are chosen at random, for fun: they are not real predictions or medical, legal or financial advice.

1. What the app collects

Kuriocie has no server and does not itself receive any data about you. The app does not ask for an account, name, email address, precise location or contacts. It contains neither a Kuriocie audience measurement tool nor artificial intelligence.

However, the app contains Google AdMob advertising: when the device is connected to the Internet, the Google Mobile Ads SDK integrated into the app collects and shares with Google the data described in section 2. The game remains usable offline; advertising and the bonus video need the Internet and do not load without a connection.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and displays advertising provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC):

  • an occasional full-screen advertisement when you return to the question selection or the home screen after an answer;
  • a banner at the bottom of the wish journal;
  • an optional video: if you watch it to the end, you receive 1 extra wish (once a day).

When the device is connected, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • the Android advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • the IP address, which may be used to estimate an approximate location;
  • interactions with the app and advertisements (launches, taps, videos watched);
  • diagnostic information (performance, crashes).

This data is used to display and measure advertisements, produce statistics and prevent fraud. It is encrypted during transfer (TLS). Google processes it under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy

Advertisements are limited to the “parental guidance recommended (PG)” rating.

Cookies and identifiers: the app does not use browser cookies, but Google may store and read equivalent identifiers on the device (advertising ID, app set ID, technical SDK identifiers) to serve advertisements, limit their repetition and measure them.

3. Your consent and choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent on first launch, using Google's consent management tool (UMP, compliant with the IAB TCF framework). Storing and reading identifiers on the device and personalised advertisements are based on this consent (GDPR, Article 6.1.a, and ePrivacy rules). If you refuse, Google may display non-personalised or limited advertisements that use less data, particularly for security and fraud prevention (legitimate interest).

You can withdraw or change your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it: menu ⓘ > “Privacy / ad choices”. Withdrawal applies to the future. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google > Ads, or Privacy > Ads).

4. Data kept on your phone

For the game to work, the app stores only on your phone: the number of wishes for the day, your streak of days, your settings (sound), the frequency of advertising displays, your wish journal (your last 30 questions and answers), the number of app launches, whether you have already dismissed the first-launch warning, the day you viewed the genie’s message, the last videos selected to avoid repeating them, and whether a one-day break was forgiven in your streak. This data is never sent to Kuriocie or advertising networks. The “Clear all” button only empties the journal on this phone. To erase all local app data, clear its data in Android settings or uninstall it.

5. Sharing

If you tap “Share”, Android opens its sharing menu with the text of your answer. You choose the app and the person who receive it. Kuriocie receives nothing.

6. Retention and deletion

Kuriocie holds no data about you and therefore cannot delete anything itself. For advertising data, use Android's advertising ID settings (reset or delete the ID), Google's tools (https://myadcenter.google.com) and, to exercise your rights (access, erasure, objection) with Google, https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can also write to Kuriocie (section 9): we will help you with the steps to take. You can lodge a complaint with the CNIL (https://www.cnil.fr).

7. Minors

The app is not intended for people under 18.

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
